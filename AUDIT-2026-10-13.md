# MCP-Dubai audit - 2026-10-13

## Result and scope

Repository audit as of **13 October 2026, Asia/Dubai**, covering changes since the 28 September audit. The strict freshness gate had failed on 10 October: `cost_of_living`, `events`, `free_zones`, `visas` and `setup_advisor` passed their 100-day full-review budget (tracking issue #8). Those five domains received a **full review**. Four medium-volatility domains that would have expired on 20 October (`dcde`, `ip_trademark`, `founder_essentials`, `data_analyst`) were also fully reviewed. `tax_compliance`, `tenancy` and `createapps`, plus the holiday dataset, received targeted refreshes. Code was reviewed for correctness, dependencies were checked for releases and advisories, and anonymous upstream integrations were called live.

The catalogue remains **120 tools, 37 features and 19 knowledge domains**; version stays **0.4.0**. Full-reviewed domains carry `knowledge_date` and `full_review_date` 2026-10-13. Targeted packs advance only `knowledge_date`.

## Correctness fixes

- **Golden Visa entrepreneur route:** ICP replaced the AED 500,000 project-valuation test (page updated 10 Sep 2026). The route is now 10 years and requires either a UAE SME with annual revenue of at least AED 1,000,000 or prior ventures sold for at least AED 7,000,000 in total. `golden_visa_check` gains `annual_revenue_aed` and `prior_exit_value_aed`. `project_value_aed` is kept for compatibility but is no longer screened; it returns a note instead. `visa_recommend` wording follows the new rule.
- **Golden Visa real estate:** the tool no longer calls mortgaged or off-plan property "unconfirmed". ICP and GDRFA accept property mortgaged with an approved bank or bought off-plan from an approved developer.
- **Free zone to mainland fees:** Article 12 of Executive Council Resolution 11 of 2025 fixes AED 10,000 per year only for a branch operating out of the free zone, and AED 5,000 for the temporary permit. An in-Emirate branch licence pays prescribed DET fees. `setup_advisor` and `free_zones.json` previously attached AED 10,000 to the in-Emirate branch licence.
- **Setup advisor:**
  - Crypto and blockchain requests on the local-trade, offshore or low-budget free zone routes no longer drop the VARA licensing warning.
  - The offshore minimum now matches the RAK ICC price in the pack (about AED 11,000, was AED 5,500).
  - Offshore next steps no longer mention Ejari or visa quota.
  - `visa_count` is now checked against each candidate zone's recorded visa limit.
- **Setup-decision plans:** `analyze_setup_decision` rejects industries that `setup_advisor` would reject, instead of returning a plan whose first step must fail.
- **Report footer:** `synthesize_report` labelled every refresh scope "targeted", including full reviews.
- **Freshness report:** the checker flagged a domain "(targeted)" whenever it had a scope text, even right after a full review. It now flags only when `full_review_date` differs from `knowledge_date`.
- **RTA GTFS helper:** returned the `www.` URL, which answered HTTP 400 to every direct request. It now returns the apex-host URL, which served the 10.4 MB archive on 3 of 4 tries. The archive is still the `GTFS_20250823` build.

## Dependencies

- **FastMCP:** the floor moves to **3.4.8** (`fastmcp>=3.4.8,<4`). 3.4.8 (4 Oct 2026) is a 3.x security release with SSE Host/Origin protection, Cookie exclusion from forwarded headers, and bounded caches. FastMCP 4.1 exists; the v4 migration remains out of scope.
- **pip-audit:** before the upgrade it flagged transitive pyjwt (via `mcp`), urllib3 (via twine) and a stray pypdf in the local environment. pyjwt is used only by OAuth paths that this stdio server does not run, and fresh installs already resolve patched versions. After upgrading the local environment, pip-audit reports **no known vulnerabilities**.
